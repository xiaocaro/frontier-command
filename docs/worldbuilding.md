# Dawn Frontier

## Premise

The player is the Admiral commanding **Dawn Frontier Command**, a Starfleet frontier administration in a non-canon post-Dominion-War timeline around 2381.

The region lies between established Federation space, the Romulan frontier and a large expanse of incompletely charted space. Starfleet is reopening the area for exploration, settlement and strategic development.

The player's objective is not to clear a map of enemies. It is to make the frontier safer, richer and better understood while managing the consequences of expansion.

## Strategic Space

The world has four broad strategic contexts:

- **Federation Rear**: secure logistics and Dawn Starbase
- **Dawn Frontier**: colonies, mines, outposts and contested routes
- **Neutral Zone / Romulan Border**: politically sensitive contact space
- **Unknown Space**: expandable sectors containing undiscovered systems, hazards and resources

Unknown space extends in multiple directions. Exploration permanently reveals generated sectors and systems; the world should not feel like a corridor from Federation to Romulan territory.

Newly catalogued systems and planets receive temporary Starfleet designations and may be named by the player. Owned ships, colonies and facilities may also be renamed.

## Dawn Starbase

Dawn Starbase is the secure strategic headquarters, not a front-line target.

Its dedicated interface contains:

- COMMAND
- SHIPYARD
- DRYDOCK
- ARMORY
- ENGINEERING
- LOGISTICS
- SENSOR CONTROL
- DEFENSE GRID

The base has heavy shields and phaser arrays; Defense Grid unlocks new frontier platforms. Ordinary raiders should treat it as an unattractive target.

Starbase converts frontier gains into new capability through ship construction, refits, repairs, ammunition, sensors, logistics and defenses.

## Federation Frontier

Colonies, mines, relays and outposts are persistent communities and infrastructure, not disposable mission props.

They possess defenses appropriate to their role and should normally survive long enough for Starfleet to respond.

Their continued existence creates strategic value:

- colonies produce Credits from population and development
- mines create materials and valuable transport routes
- relays extend logistics and sensors
- outposts improve warning and frontier presence

Expansion creates both more income and more territory to protect.

## Core Ecology

The frontier generates its own MMO content:

**EXPLORE → DISCOVER → MINE → TRANSPORT → CREATE VALUE → ATTRACT THREATS → DEFEND → PROFIT → GROW → EXPLORE FARTHER**

The four core activities share one world:

### Combat / Security

Patrol, escort, intercept, shadow, attack, disable, drive off and retreat. Combat affects real ships, facilities, cargo and political tension.

### Exploration / Survey

Unknown systems, planets, anomalies and resource sites are genuinely discovered. Remote and close survey trade safety for information and reward.

### Mining / Extraction

Persistent resource sites produce physical materials. Mine facilities extract finite deposits into limited local storage. Exposure, accidents and physical transport determine output. Vessels cannot replace the mine production chain.

### Transport / Logistics

Real goods move between real inventories. Route choice trades travel time against known and unknown risk.

Activities should create opportunities for each other instead of behaving as independent task lists.

## Admiral Authority

Every Starfleet ship remains available to the Admiral at all times.

Any ship may attempt any physically valid activity. Specialization changes efficiency and risk, not permission.

The Admiral may replace, queue or interrupt a ship's directive. Explicit commands override standing autonomous policy after validation.

ROE governs what a captain may do without new orders; it does not prevent the Admiral from deliberately ordering an attack and accepting the consequences.

## Orion Syndicate

The frontier's pirate/criminal faction is the **Orion Syndicate**.

It has persistent hideouts, ships, materials, ammunition, cargo and intelligence.

Orion activity is economic:

- scout profitable routes
- identify weak escorts or facilities
- raid real cargo
- withdraw when risk becomes unacceptable
- return stolen goods to a hideout
- rebuild only when resources allow

Destroyed Orion ships are actual losses. A retreating raider can be followed to discover its base. Orion pressure therefore emerges from the player's own frontier economy rather than a spawn timer.

## Romulan Border Command

Romulans are a political and military faction, not generic enemies.

Scouts observe Federation expansion and report what they actually learn. Border Command may respond by observing, patrolling, probing or escalating depending on intelligence, tension, risk and strategic value.

A Romulan vessel is not automatically a legal hostile target. Shadow, intercept, attack and withdrawal are distinct choices with persistent diplomatic consequences.

## Growth

Growth has two main forms.

### Fleet Development

Ships use limited module slots. Useful upgrades unlock new options such as deep scanning, long-range detection, precision subsystem targeting, stronger hazardous-route survival or expanded cargo.

### Frontier Development

Starbase and frontier infrastructure unlock wider strategic options through better sensors, shipbuilding, armory capacity, logistics and defenses.

Growth should change what the player can do in the next activity, not merely add passive percentage bonuses.

## Economy

- **Credits**: strategic budget
- **Materials**: construction, shipbuilding and repair
- **Photon / Quantum Torpedoes**: physical ammunition
- **Special Finds**: rare discoveries enabling unique capabilities

Exploration, trade, industry and security all feed the same economy.

## Future Agents

Future Agents will live inside this existing frontier rather than receive artificial task menus.

They will observe real opportunities—escort demand, mining sites, unexplored systems, threats, logistics needs—and choose actions according to skills, personality, memory and relationships.

The world must remain interesting and causally coherent even before LLM Agents are added.

## V1 / Lv1 + Lv2 Playable Rules

This build retains the existing LCARS console language and star-date presentation. It is a non-canon sandbox rather than an assertion about historical Star Trek events.

Dawn sits at world (-240, 0), behind the frontier. Initial Federation assets are New Horizon colony, Helios mine and Beacon outpost. Each has an independent shield, hull, reactor and phaser; Dawn has heavy self-defense. Defense Grid adds platforms and strengthens Federation facility fire. New outposts add defended sensor/logistics route nodes, mines consume finite deposits, and colonies produce Credits; medical actions consume budget and construction consumes physical Materials.

Unknown sectors form a sparse 400-unit grid in several directions. Each seed generates persistent systems, planets, normal anomalies and deposits, plus hidden anomalies unlocked by Deep Scan. Remote survey gives charted outlines; close survey confirms usable deposits, habitability and hazards. A normal anomaly's special sample can be collected once into cargo, then physically unloaded for an upgrade.

Safe logistics goes directly to the ordered destination when no observed danger requires avoidance. Facilities are destinations or services, not compulsory waypoints. Only charted anomalies and fresh hostile contacts may cause a deterministic detour. Direct travel preserves uncertainty about uncharted space. Risky corridors trade a faster warp passage for physical turbulence and a stronger detectable signature while underway. Admiral route forecasts show the uncharted fraction from known geography, rather than suggesting certainty about hidden threats.

The initial Orion hideout supports one scout and two raiders, finite stock and magazines. The scout inspects known frontier traffic and follows valuable locally observed cargo; it does not know all Federation assets. Raider opportunities depend on fresh reports, freight/stock value, distance and escort strength. Theft harms the actual source inventory, and the resulting cargo must survive the return journey. A quarter of returned goods is consumed by black-market export; the remainder supports repairs and industry. Destroying assets or the hideout changes its ability to act. Shadow can follow a returning raider to the source of this pressure.

The initial Romulan scout reports to Border Command, which deploys a patrol warbird and a preexisting reserve. A neutral identity stays outside autonomous hostile selection. Crossing the Neutral Zone adds tension, entering Romulan space adds more, and withdrawal reduces it; deliberate fire on an unconfirmed Romulan creates a larger political change. Construction near the border also matters. Fresh intelligence and tension permit observe, patrol, probe or military escalation; escalation is a major communication and pause.

Fleet roles are performance profiles. A Peregrine can survey or haul small batches; an Antares can deliberately attack with its phaser. Expanded Cargo changes batch size; Long Range Sensors changes contact/exploration reach; Deep Scan reveals otherwise absent opportunities; Precision Targeting changes the available disabling targets; Reinforced Shields supports exposed travel. Losses remain permanent, while actual Shipyard construction, colony income and finite paid merchant deliveries allow recovery.

There are no Science, Intel or Allocation currencies, mission payouts for moving the same goods, or menus that manufacture an enemy target. The OPERATIONS view reads discoverable sectors, transport demand, valuable inventory, construction demand and observed threats. Hail, warnings, withdrawal demands and persistent diplomatic incidents use rules. LLM personality decisions and formal online multiplayer remain future work.

## Society and events

New Horizon begins with 1000 residents and its own Commander. New colonies begin with 250. Population, housing, stability, morale, security, industry and science persist. Credits production every five game minutes provides the command budget; housing, industry, science and security development require local Materials and budget. Cadet training and appointments affect rules while leaving Admiral authority unchanged.

Wormholes create actual paired routes. Plague reduces population, morale and production, while quarantine trades income for containment. Mining accidents halt a real facility until on-site action repairs it. Distress originates from actual damage. Refugee transport preserves source population, carrier occupancy and destination changes. Investigating ruins or derelicts yields finite discoveries, cargo or contamination follow-ups. Every event keeps its evidence and consequences after the communication scrolls away.

## Stealth frontier

A fixed Dawn Passage in FRONTIER 0/0 leads to deterministic sector 0/12 and its unique abandoned Veil reconnaissance vessel. Survey and on-site event assistance establish the permanent ship registry. It has minimal combat power and high passive sensors; cloak costs less than its core regeneration. Recovery can be delayed without losing the vessel, while destruction after recovery remains permanent.

Orion and Romulan ships can recognize sustained physically observed followers, then take decoy routes and detours until the detected tail disappears. Romulan counter-cloak detection is stronger. Veil's low signature permits secret following. Unknown destination facilities remain unidentified signals until ten minutes of continuous local observation; an enemy's internal home or docked state never grants a discovery.

Dawn's mine capacity is 3000 Materials; Colony produces Credits. Facilities have no recurring maintenance fee. Owned ships default to holding position; emergency disengagement ends locally, and return to base requires an Admiral directive. The map palette distinguishes Federation cyan, Dawn amber, Romulan green, Orion yellow-green and fixed body-type colors.

## v9 Admiral response

Every newly observed external vessel raises a contact alert and pauses at its real detection tick. Romulan identity does not establish hostility. Acknowledging or locating the contact and issuing orders keep the world paused until the Admiral resumes. Continuous observation is deduplicated; ten minutes without signal or first confirmed hostility can warrant another alert.

Surveyed Veil recovery is an immediate contextual opportunity, using the same persistent event and on-site assistance. Contextual next steps expose exploration, construction and actual delivery requirements without inventing automatic missions. Claimed or emptied wreck markers can be dismissed while their entities and historical consequences remain in the save.
